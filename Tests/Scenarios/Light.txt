# Light and darkness: the light circle of 2D maps with changing light (graveyard, map 65),
# dark 3D maps (Breth's cellar, map 72), light spells and blindness.
waittext
dismissall
# Satine (gray wizard) knows light 2 and always succeeds
join 62
setvalue 2 usemagic 100
# --- at day everything is visible
hour 16
light light
# --- at night only a small circle around the party is lit (the radius shrinks slowly)
settime 22 0
wait 2000
light 16
# --- light 2 makes the circle larger by 4 * 8 pixels
activate 2
click 230 170 right
button 4
waitscreen SpellSelect
spell Gray 2
waitidle
wait 2000
light 48
# --- the light spell is gone (e.g. by an anti-magic field)
setactivespell Light 0
wait 2000
light 16
# --- a dark 3D map is completely black without light
teleport 72 10 17
dismissall
light dark
button 4
waitscreen SpellSelect
spell Gray 2
waitidle
wait 100
light light
# --- a blind active party member can't see anything
setcondition 2 blind 1
wait 100
light dark
setcondition 2 blind 0
wait 100
light light
