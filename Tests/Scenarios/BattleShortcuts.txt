# Shortcuts of the port in battles (not in the original): a click on a free cell of the party rows which can be
# reached chooses the move, a click on a monster which can be attacked chooses the attack (monster group 1: a giant rat).
# The tactical battle field is at 208,49 with cells of 16 pixels.
waittext
dismissall
setvalue 1 Attack 100
startbattle 1
waitscreen Battle
# --- move to the cell 2,3
click 248 105
battleround
battlecell 2 3 THALION
battlecell 2 2 RIESENRATTE
cleartexts
# --- attack the rat at 2,2
click 248 89
battleround
animationshown damage RIESENRATTE
